Settings

Stored in /config/vumovie.json (mounted volume). Environment variables always win.

“ffmpeg diagnostics” times ffmpeg -version, shows what the container sees under /dev/dri and runs a 2 s VAAPI encode per installed driver (iHD, i965) — that is the fastest way to tell a slow disk from a missing binary from a broken driver.
