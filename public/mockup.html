Every candidate URL is probed with ffprobe before it is offered — dead mirrors are skipped, exactly like a player would. Chunked HLS/DASH is detected here and reported as “N chunks → 1 stream”.

Stream & transcode

One stable LAN URL per title. VLC, the Duo2 and a browser can all hit the same URL at the same time.

selected: server 1 · cinejoy CDN-1 1080p H.264source chunks: 214unified → MPEG-TS
2 clients
How the “chunks → 1 stream” part works — the app is a real proxy: it pulls the upstream HLS/DASH segments as fast as the client needs them and pushes one continuous MPEG-TS/HLS stream out. Mode: stream-copy when the source is already ≤1080p H.264 VAAPI transcode when it is 4K / HEVC / when you force it. No CPU is wasted on remuxing, the NAS never has to buffer a whole film, and the upstream URL (which usually expires in minutes) is never exposed to VLC or the Duo2.

Transcode profile

Left untouched when off and the source is already fine — remux only, ~0% CPU.

1080p on a J3455: H.264 encode at 1080p is possible, but keep one transcode at a time and stay near the bitrates above. H.265 encode is not available in this hardware.

Push to VU+ Duo2

            

              
Upload userbouquet.vumovie.tv to /etc/enigma2/ (WebIF upload or SFTP).

              
Ensure the bouquet is referenced in bouquets.tv (added once, kept on later pushes).

              
Trigger GET /web/servicelistreload?mode=2 and verify with /web/getservices.

              
Fallback if WebIF is disabled: write the same two files into a mounted SMB/NFS share and let your box’s script copy them.
